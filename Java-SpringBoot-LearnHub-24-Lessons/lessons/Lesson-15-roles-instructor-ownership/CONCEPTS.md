# Lesson 15 — Instructor ownership

## Deep dive

Study `CurrentActor; CourseOwnershipPolicy` and how they enforce **object-level ownership**. Differentiate authentication, authorization, transport contracts, and application-service policy. Model direct HTTP attackers rather than trusting Angular route guards.

## Failure scenarios

Consider anonymous requests, CSRF omissions, cross-user IDs, stale sessions, and data leaks. Document expected status codes and persistence behavior.
